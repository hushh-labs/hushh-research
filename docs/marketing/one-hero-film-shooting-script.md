# 🤫 One — Hero Film Shooting Script: *"Sixty Seconds"*

> **Your agents. Yours to own.**

**TL;DR:** A complete, shoot-ready pitch film for the One launch — the emotional flagship that makes people *feel* the category before they understand it. Opens on the quiet harm that started Hussh, turns to hope, lands the 60-Second Challenge with consent shown on camera, and closes on the punchline. Selected launch direction: the **consumer hero, voiceover + product only** (no people on camera) — emotion carried by chaos resolving into calm. Also included: a 90-second reference cut with people, and a one-take "shoot-it-this-weekend" founder version. Clean teleprompter VO at the end.

Companion to the [One Launch Content Strategy](./one-content-strategy.md). All financial / One Puppy cuts marked **INTERNAL** stay internal-only until McDermott clears them. Everything else is built to survive legal review.

---

## The idea in one line

> *A computer that is finally, completely yours — and the sixty seconds where you feel it.*

We don't win hearts by listing features. We win them by showing a person have **one calm, slightly magical minute** with something that is loyal only to them — and showing, on camera, that it only ever touched what they pointed at. The feeling we're chasing is **relief**.

---

## Why this wins hearts (the emotional architecture)

| Beat | Emotion | Craft move |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | Recognition / unease | The universal 2am "your account was accessed" dread — restrained, not scary |
| **Turn** | Hope | "We built One so that never happens to you again" |
| **The minute** | Delight + trust | The 60-Second Challenge: One *asks*, the person *approves*, One *acts* |
| **Ownership** | Pride / calm | It ran on your machine, your key, your private ledger. 🐶 fetches only what you point at |
| **Punchline** | Belonging | *Your agents. Yours to own.* — an identity, not a tagline |

The whole film is **restraint as the flex**. Apple doesn't shout. Neither do we.

## ★ SELECTED CUT — Consumer Hero, *Voiceover + Product Only* (no people on camera)

**This is the chosen direction for the launch hero.** No actors, no founder face — the whole film is the device, the interface, motion type, the 🐶 mark, light, and one warm voice. The emotion comes from **chaos resolving into calm.** This is the most Apple, most premium cut we can ship. ~80 seconds.

**Format:** macro product cinematography of a real iPhone/Mac, screen content as pristine motion graphics (SF Pro, Liquid Glass, dark-mode). No hands needed; if used, a single calm hand only. Cold-blue → warm-gold light arc. One piano motif, unresolved until the punchline.

## THE ONE-TAKE VERSION — shoot it this weekend 🎥

For when you want something *real and human* now — founder or a Champion, one phone or one Mac, no crew. Authenticity beats polish for the first drop.

**Setup:** Natural window light. Phone at eye level. You, your actual phone, a quiet room. One honest take. ~60 seconds.

**The shot:** You, talking to camera like a friend, then turning to do the 60-Second Challenge live on your own device, then back to camera for the line.

**Beats:**
1. **(to camera, 0:00–0:12)** "Hey. Quick story, then I'll show you the thing. A while back, someone stole my identity, and my family got hurt before I even knew. That's the whole reason this exists."
2. **(to camera, 0:12–0:20)** "So we made One — an agent that's loyal to exactly one person. You. Let me just show you. Sixty seconds."
3. **(turn to your phone, 0:20–0:48)** Do it for real. Ask One for help with one thing this week. Let the consent card and the receipt show on screen-record. React honestly.
4. **(back to camera, 0:48–0:58)** "That's it. It only saw what I pointed at. It ran on my phone, with my key. Nobody else."
5. **(0:58–1:04)** Hold a beat. Smile. "Your agents. Yours to own." 🤫

**Why this one wins:** it's the founder, the true origin, and a real product moment in one breath. People forward *that.*

## Production notes (so it looks like Apple shipped it)

- **Casting:** real, warm, un-actory faces. One advisor, one parent, one business owner — diverse, calm, no "stock smile." The relief on the face at 1:06 is the most important frame in the film; cast for it.
- **Design:** SF Pro type, Liquid Glass cards, generous HIG spacing, dark-mode hero. One idea per frame. The cards animate in with restraint — no swooshes.
- **Color/light:** cold-blue dread in the cold open → warm, golden, lived-in light from the turn onward. The light *is* the story.
- **Music:** a single warm piano motif (one instrument, one theme) that stays unresolved until the punchline, then lands on the home chord exactly as "Yours to own" types in. Score the silence as much as the notes.
- **The consent beat is non-negotiable on camera.** If the ask-approve-audit card isn't visible, we don't have the film — we have an ad for surveillance, which is the opposite of us.
- **Pace:** unhurried. Let the 1:06 beat *breathe* for a full second. Confidence is quiet.
- **Voice:** the VO is the founder, or a warm, low, present read. Brief. Never announcer-y.
