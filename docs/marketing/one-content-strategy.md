# 🤫 One — Launch Content Strategy

> **Your agents. Yours to own.**

**TL;DR:** A consent-first content engine for the launch of **One** and the **🐶 One Puppy** line. The whole strategy hangs on one feeling — *a computer that is finally, completely yours, working for you, owned by you* — told through a repeatable hero franchise (**The 60-Second Challenge**), a lovable cast (One Puppy and the circle of agents), and a human distribution community (Agents → Owners). It is built to be fun, gorgeous, and true: every claim survives legal review, and every piece reinforces that the person — never Hussh, never a partner — owns the intelligence.

This document is the durable content north star for the One / One Puppy launch. It does **not** approve any specific asset for external publication — One Puppy marketing remains internal-only pending McDermott review (see [Guardrails](#guardrails--the-hard-nevers)). It defines *what we say, how we say it, and the lines we never cross.*

---

## 0. Two course-corrections (read first)

The launch brief contains two ideas that, taken literally, would damage the brand and create legal exposure. The strategy below deliberately replaces both. This is not me softening the vision — it is protecting it.

### 0.1 The "60-second challenge" is about what One does *for you*, not a confession booth

The brief imagines onboarding as the user pouring out everything intimate — relationships, sex life, exes, who they trust and distrust. **We do not do that, and we never market it.** A company whose entire reason to exist is *"never again should private context move without you"* cannot open the relationship by hoovering a person's most sensitive secrets door-to-door. That is the exact harm Hussh was founded to end.

The product truth is already better and already public: **"sixty seconds of listening, then One acts."** The 60-Second Challenge is *"tell One what you want handled,"* not *"tell One your secrets."* One earns context the way a trusted person does — slowly, with consent, only what's needed for the job in front of it, every access written to a private ledger only the owner can read. **The flex is restraint.** That is the more premium, more Apple, more *true* story — and it's the one this strategy tells.

### 0.2 The "Partners Verified Program" must never claim Apple / Amex / Costco / Google as partners

The brief lists Apple One, Google One, Costco Executive, Amex Centurion, airline status, etc. as a "One Partners Verified Program." **Hussh has no affiliation, endorsement, or partnership with any of these companies**, and saying otherwise is a trademark and false-association problem. We say the true, stronger thing instead: **One is BYOA — Bring Your Own AI / API / compute / subscriptions.** If you *already* pay for great hardware and services, One puts your agent on top of what you own and already love. The named brands appear only as *things you already own*, wrapped in the standard disclaimer — never as "partners," never as a co-branded "verified" tier.

Everything downstream is built on these two corrections.

## 3. Voice & brand non-negotiables (quick reference)

Lifted from the constitutional rules — these are not style preferences, they are invariants.

- **Spelling:** Always **Hussh** (double-s). Always **One** capitalized. The mark is **🤫**.
- **Punchline (fixed):** *🤫 Your agents. Yours to own.*
- **Tagline arc (in order):** Phase 1 *"your data, your business"* → Phase 2 *"your agents, your business"* → Phase 3 *"your agents, yours to Own 🤫."* Don't pull Phase 3 language into Phase 1 copy.
- **Design language:** Apple Summer 2026 — SF Pro typography, Liquid Glass material, Apple palette, iOS-first layout, HIG spacing. Dark mode is the hero canvas.
- **Decision frame:** *What would Apple do?* Run every concept through it. Taste, restraint, one idea per asset.
- **Discretionary numbers** bias to **.69** ($0.69 reserve, 16.9% referral, the One Puppy ladder).
- **Tone:** warm, present, brief, a little playful — never breathless, never fear-mongering, never "privacy is cute." Make trust *explicit*, not adorable.
- **One is not a chatbot.** The surface is **voice + glanceable cards**, not a chat log. Show that in every demo.

## 6. Hero franchise: **The 60-Second Challenge** 🎬

The repeatable, ownable, fun format the whole launch hangs on.

**The premise:** A person sits down with One for the first time. A 60-second timer starts. They simply *talk* — "Here's what I'd love handled this week." One listens, asks for consent on each scope, and by the end produces one genuinely useful, glanceable result. The reveal lands with a real number or a real action, not a data dump.

**Two flavors:**

- **Consumer cut:** "I just want my life a little more handled." → One surfaces a calendar conflict resolved, a bill spotted, a thing remembered — with consent receipts shown on screen.
- **One Puppy / RIA cut (internal-only until gates clear):** the canonical close — *connect one account, see your complete financial picture with a dollar figure attached, in sixty seconds.* This is "the demo is the close." The dollar-figure reveal is the money shot.

**Why it works as content:** built-in timer = tension; "listening, then acting" = payoff; infinitely remixable across personas; trivially turned into a creator/UGC challenge ("Take the 60-Second Challenge"). It is the franchise that makes the category legible.

**The visible consent beat is mandatory.** Every Challenge piece must show One *asking* and the owner *approving* on-screen, plus the private-ledger receipt. The consent moment isn't friction to hide — it's the brand, on camera. *Ask. Approve. Audit.* (hu_ssh: SSH for humans.)

## 7. The cast (make it lovable)

Apple sells silicon by making it feel like a character. We do the same with the agents.

- **🐶 One Puppy** — the hero mascot of the launch SKU. *"Fetches your data, guards your house."* Loyal, eager, never barks at strangers it shouldn't. The dog is the single most shareable asset we have — lean into it: the puppy that fetches *only what you point at*, and sits on everything else.
- **One** — the calm relationship layer. Not a face, a presence. Voice + cards.
- **Kai** — finance specialist. Warm, present, brief. Shows up when money is the job. (Keep separate from any Fund A messaging — different thread, different deck.)
- **Nav** — the privacy guardian. Precise, composed, audit-grade. *The specialist that helps you and the guardian that protects you are not the same authority.* Nav is how we make consent feel like a feature, not a settings page.

Content rule: **one agent, one job, one scene.** Never collapse them into "an AI assistant." The naming discipline *is* the moat.

## 9. The 90-day launch calendar (phased to the tagline arc)

Discipline: **phase claims must match where the product actually is.** We move through the tagline arc; we do not jump ahead.

### Phase 1 — *"Your data, your business."* (Weeks 1–4: Curiosity)
- **Goal:** Establish the category and the feeling; collect reservations.
- **Hero drop:** Founder origin film (P3) — the identity-theft moral contract, told with restraint. *This is why One exists.*
- **Franchise launch:** First 3 consumer 60-Second Challenge cuts (P1).
- **Cast intro:** Meet 🐶 One Puppy (P2) — the fetch-and-guard explainer.
- **CTA:** *Reserve for $0.69.*

### Phase 2 — *"Your agents, your business."* (Weeks 5–9: Comprehension)
- **Goal:** Make ownership and the agents legible; activate pros.
- **Ownership series:** "Ask. Approve. Audit." carousels + the private-ledger explainer (P3).
- **For Pros:** "One agent. Every door in." — the RIA craft series (P4), internal-review-gated demos.
- **Community:** First Agent/Champion spotlights (P5) — the craft of bringing One to a door.
- **Cast:** Nav and Kai one-job explainers (P2).

### Phase 3 — *"Your agents, yours to Own 🤫."* (Weeks 10–13: Conviction → Launch)
- **Goal:** Land the punchline; convert reservations to owners; open the ecosystem.
- **Keynote-grade hero:** The full One film — category, agents, consent, ecosystem (Apple Summer 2026 design throughout).
- **Challenge, at scale:** Open "Take the 60-Second Challenge" as public UGC; creator duets.
- **Ecosystem:** Partner co-build / co-sell stories (P5) with the standard independence disclaimer.
- **CTA:** Own One.

> Cadence target: 1 hero piece + 3–4 short-form + 2 social/cast + 1 community piece per week. Quality over volume — *would Apple ship this?* is the gate on every asset.

## 12. Guardrails — the hard nevers

Every asset clears these before it leaves the building. (Sources: constitutional rules; One Puppy product page; partner-ecosystem disclaimer.)

1. **Never claim a partnership/affiliation/endorsement** with Apple, Google, Microsoft, Costco, American Express, NVIDIA, Samsung, Dell, HP, Lenovo, airlines, or any named company. They appear only as platforms One runs on or things you already own, always with the independence disclaimer. **No "Partners Verified" co-brand tier.**
2. **One Puppy marketing is internal-only** until McDermott (Michael Brown) clears trademark/usage + Marketing-Rule adjacency. No external One Puppy financial demo, price, or performance claim before sign-off.
3. **No performance or compliance claims** before counsel sign-off. The RIA story is *on-device + transparency log reduces vendor risk* — never "compliant," never returns.
4. **Never market the intimate-data confession.** Onboarding content shows One earning *task-scoped* context with visible consent — not sex lives, exes, or "tell us everything."
5. **Keep Fund A entirely separate.** No content bundles One/Kai product messaging with any fund or investment-return narrative. Different thread, different deck.
6. **Show consent on camera, always.** Any "Listens/Acts" claim must depict the ask-approve-audit beat and the private ledger. No silent data flows, even in a mockup.
7. **Phase discipline:** don't pull Phase 3 punchline language into Phase 1 copy.
8. **Spelling & marks:** Hussh (double-s), One (capital), 🤫 — every time, everywhere.
9. **No credential claims** beyond the true operating record (product leadership at Google, Microsoft, Splunk). No Stanford GSB/MBA references.
10. **"What would Apple do?"** is the final taste gate. If Apple wouldn't ship it, we don't.
