# Email domain marks

Curated company and mail-provider favicon artwork, downloaded from Google’s public favicon cache on 2026-10-08. Each PNG corresponds to the primary domain in `lib/profile/email-domain-brands.ts`. Hushh reuses the existing local brand mark. These assets identify email domains for presentation; they are not affiliation or verification badges. No email address or domain is sent to an external service at runtime. Brand names and artwork belong to their respective owners.
