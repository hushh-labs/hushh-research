# Working typing poses

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool using
`public/one-presence/drafting-hd.png` as the identity and pose reference.
The output is a transparent 1254px square, arranged as sixteen equal cells.
This is the authored input; regenerate deployed files through
`scripts/design/build-one-working-presence.mjs` rather than editing binaries.

Prompt:

> Create a production ANIMATION SPRITE SHEET from the supplied gold One mascot
> reference. Output a SQUARE image with EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows of equal
> square cells, 16 full-body consecutive animation keyframes, ordered
> left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Truly transparent alpha background. NO grid
> lines, borders, labels, text, shadows on ground, checkerboard or extra props.
> Preserve the EXACT glossy golden character identity, dark oval eyes, small
> oval mouth, charcoal over-ear headphones, laptop, lighting, camera angle and
> body proportions in the reference. In EVERY cell, same scale, same standing
> foot positions, full headband and BOTH FEET fully visible with 10 percent
> padding, character centered identically. Laptop remains supported by its
> left hand. Animate an energetic clearly readable working/typing loop: its
> RIGHT hand visibly lifts 5-8 percent of body height off the keyboard, fingers
> spread slightly, moves forward, taps keyboard and rises again, doing TWO
> complete typing bursts across the 16 keyframes, with a subtle head nod toward
> the laptop, then a short natural blink and a focused return. Let frame1 be
> neutral hand on keys, frames2-4 hand lift, frames5-7 pressing down and typing,
> frame8 hand resting, frames9-11 second visible lift, frames12-14 pressing
> down, frame15 blink, frame16 returning toward frame1. Movement should read
> clearly even displayed as a small app icon. This must be visibly different
> hand poses, not the same photo copied16 times. Stable laptop/headphone
> geometry, stable body silhouette, no bouncing/floating legs, no duplicated
> hands, no changing camera, no character redesign. All16 frames equally sized
> and uniformly registered so looping them makes a clean typing animation.
> Do not crop any character at cell edges.
